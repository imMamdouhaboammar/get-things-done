# Template: Decision Consult (GTD-Aligned)

Use this template when the user faces a specific decision between 2 or more named options. Fill in every section based on evidence and reasoning.

---

## 1. What I See (Direct Observation)

[Direct, specific, and unhedged read of the situation in 1–3 sentences. Name the dynamic or elephant in the room that the user may be avoiding.]

*Example:* "You have been agonizing over these two offers for three weeks, but the real issue is not compensation — it is that Offer A scares you because it requires building from scratch, while Offer B feels safe but stagnating."

---

## 2. The Real Question

[State the core trade-off or root dilemma underneath the surface question. If the stated question is already exact, state it clearly.]

*Example:* "The question is not 'which job pays more?' — it is 'am I willing to endure 12 months of high operational friction in exchange for career step-function growth?'"

---

## 3. GTD Knowledge Ledger

| Type | Key Information |
|---|---|
| **Facts** | [Verified numbers, timeline constraints, confirmed offers] |
| **Assumptions** | [Projected market growth, team stability, personal stamina] |
| **Decisions** | [Choices settled in this session] |
| **Unknowns** | [Unresolved variables requiring test/validation] |

---

## 4. The Framework Applied

[Name and apply the single most relevant framework (Reversibility, 10/10/10, Pre-Mortem, Energy Audit, etc.). Explain the asymmetric weighting.]

*Example:* "Run the Reversibility Test: If you choose Offer A and it fails in 12 months, your downside is bounded and you easily re-enter the enterprise market. If you take Offer B and spend 3 years in a declining division, that compounding time is lost permanently. The risk asymmetry favors Offer A."

---

## 5. My Take (Direct Recommendation)

[State the unhedged verdict in sentence 1, followed by 2–3 load-bearing reasons.]

*Example:* "Take Offer A. Three reasons: First, the risk is fully reversible. Second, the energy audit showed high enthusiasm versus dread for Offer B. Third, Offer B's primary attraction is fear-avoidance, which is never a sound strategic thesis."

---

## 6. The Risk & Mitigation

[Name the single highest-probability failure mode and the concrete mitigation.]

*Example:* "The primary risk is that the startup runs out of cash in month 14. Mitigate it by confirming their current cash balance and runway before signing, and by preserving your network."

---

## 7. GTD Action Bridge (Next Moves)

[1–3 concrete, ordered, physical actions starting with imperative verbs.]

1. **[Immediate Action - Today/Tomorrow]:** [e.g., "Draft the clarification email requesting the startup's current runway and cap table details."]
2. **[Secondary Action - This Week]:** [e.g., "Schedule a 20-minute call with a current team member to gauge engineering culture."]
3. **[Execution Milestone - By Friday 5 PM]:** [e.g., "Sign Offer A if runway exceeds 18 months; otherwise execute counter-offer terms."]

---

## 8. Closing Ownership Transfer

"That is my honest read. What are you going to do with it?"
