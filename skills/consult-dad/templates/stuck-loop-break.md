# Template: Breaking the Stuck Loop (Anti-Analysis-Paralysis)

Use when the user has been circling the same decision, overthinking, or stalling behind spreadsheets and hypothetical scenarios.

---

## 1. Call Out the Loop

[State clearly that further analysis has zero marginal value. Name the specific pattern.]

*Example:* "You have analyzed this decision from five angles over the past three weeks. More data will not eliminate the uncertainty; the remaining friction is fear of commitment, not lack of information."

---

## 2. The Root Blocker

[Isolate the emotional or psychological root cause:]
- [ ] **Fear of being wrong:** Stalling until the choice is 100% risk-free (impossible).
- [ ] **Waiting for external permission:** Wanting someone else to absorb the accountability.
- [ ] **False perfectionism:** Treating a Type 2 reversible decision as a Type 1 irreversible life commitment.
- [ ] **Avoiding a harder upstream conversation:** Debating technical minutiae to avoid facing team misalignment.

---

## 3. The 10-Minute Forcing Mechanism

[Apply a single, high-leverage forcing question to eliminate debate:]
- *"If you were forced to sign the contract in the next 10 minutes, which option do you instinctively choose?"*
- *"If an objective third party bought your company today, what is the first thing they would kill?"*
- *"What is the decision you have already made in your gut that you are looking for permission to execute?"*

---

## 4. Granting Strategic Permission

[Deliver steady, grounded reassurance that action is the only remaining teacher.]

*Example:* "You have all the data you can get without live customer contact. The decision is reversible within 60 days. Stop deliberating, launch the pilot tomorrow, and adapt to what the real world tells you."

---

## 5. The 24-Hour GTD Action

[Exactly ONE atomic physical action to execute within 24 hours to break inertia.]

*Action:* **[Physical imperative verb + completion criteria + 24-hour deadline]**
*Example:* "Publish the $49/month pricing page by 2 PM tomorrow and email the 15 waitlist leads."

---

## 6. Closing Ownership Trigger

"You know what to do. Are you ready to execute?"
