# Template: Honest Feedback on a Plan or Proposal

Use when the user presents a plan, idea, architecture, pitch, or strategy and asks for an honest critique.

---

## 1. What Works (The Structural Strengths)

[Identify 1–2 specific, genuine strengths of the plan. No flattery — state the exact mechanisms that are sound.]

- **[Strength 1]:** [e.g., "The modular rollout minimizes upfront migration risk."]
- **[Strength 2]:** [e.g., "The target customer pain point is acute and well-defined."]

---

## 2. The Real Problem (The Fatal or High-Risk Flaw)

[Name the single biggest flaw or vulnerability. Be completely direct and specific about the causal chain.]

*Observation:* [e.g., "Your go-to-market plan relies entirely on viral word-of-mouth with zero paid or outbound acquisition engine, in an enterprise B2B vertical that does not share tools publicly."]
*Consequence:* [e.g., "If uncorrected, you will burn 6 months of runway building a great product that no buyer ever discovers."]

---

## 3. GTD Knowledge Breakdown

- **Load-Bearing Fact:** [e.g., "Current CAC = $0, organic signups = 3/month."]
- **Dangerous Assumption:** [e.g., "Assumes enterprise IT directors will tweet about internal database tools."]
- **Critical Blocker:** [e.g., "No direct sales pipeline or target account list."]

---

## 4. What Must Change

[Specific, concrete modification to fix the critical vulnerability.]

*Correction:* [e.g., "Replace the viral growth hypothesis with a direct outbound pilot program: manually contact 30 target IT directors using cold outreach and personalized Loom demos."]

---

## 5. Verdict

[Choose one clear summary statement:]
- **[ ] Green (Ready to Execute):** Execute as designed; risks are bounded.
- **[ ] Yellow (Fix Before Execution):** Solid core; fix [Critical Flaw] before investing capital or engineering time.
- **[ ] Red (Do Not Execute as Designed):** Structural assumptions are invalid; rethink the foundational premise.

---

## 6. GTD Action Bridge

1. **[Immediate Action]:** [e.g., "Draft a list of 25 ICP accounts and their heads of infrastructure."]
2. **[Validation Action]:** [e.g., "Send 10 personalized outreach messages testing the core value proposition."]
3. **[Go/No-Go Gate]:** [e.g., "If 2+ accounts agree to a demo call by Friday, proceed with pilot build; otherwise refine the pitch."]

---

## 7. Closing Question

"That is the honest assessment. How do you want to proceed?"
