# GTD Domain Pack: Research
## Selection signals
Research questions, literature review, market research, technical investigation, evidence synthesis, source verification, comparative analysis, feasibility study
## Domain vocabulary
Keep distinct: question, hypothesis, claim, evidence, source, method, sample, uncertainty, limitation, confounder, inference, conclusion
## Diagnostic questions
Ask only when blocking: What decision will the research inform? What would change your mind? What source quality is required? What period and population matter? Is the goal discovery, estimation, comparison, explanation, or verification?
## Extra brief fields
`research_question`, `decision_context`, `hypotheses`, `source_criteria`, `method`, `population`, `time_window`, `limitations`, `confidence`
## Readiness additions
- research question can be answered or bounded
- source quality and freshness requirements are explicit
- method fits the requested inference
- stopping criterion is defined
## Workstream patterns
- evidence review: question -> search -> source screening -> extraction -> synthesis -> limitations
- comparison: criteria -> candidates -> evidence -> normalization -> comparison -> recommendation
- feasibility: claim -> constraints -> evidence/prototype -> failure conditions -> conclusion
## Review additions
Check source authority, freshness, selection bias, contradictory evidence, causal overreach, population mismatch, uncertainty, and whether the conclusion follows from evidence
## Completion checks
Material claims have traceable evidence, contradictory evidence is represented, limitations are explicit, the answer is bounded to the researched population and timeframe, and the downstream decision is clear
## Common traps
Collecting sources without a decision question, confusing source count with quality, hiding contradictions, overstating causal claims, and researching past the decision threshold
